HOW TO PLAY

1 · DRIVE
  • W↑Accelerate
  • AD←→Steer
  • S↓Brake / reverse
  • SPACEDrift (hold) · Shift too
  • EUse item
  • 1–4Camera views
  • MSound on / off
  • ESCMenu

On a phone or iPad the gas is on automatically. Steer with the stick under your left thumb (pull it down to brake). Your right thumb holds DRIFT and taps ITEM.

2 · DRIFT FOR TURBOS

Your speed comes from drifting. Going into a corner, hold SPACE while steering. The kart hops into a slide and its sparks change colour the longer you hold it. Let go to fire a turbo.

  • BLUE TURBO
  • ORANGE SUPER TURBO
  • PINK ULTRA TURBO

Steering harder into the corner charges the turbo faster. Getting hit while you drift throws the turbo away.

3 · ON THE TRACK
  • Boost pads: glowing arrows on the straights. Drive over one for a free burst of speed.
  • Item bubbles: rows of floating bubbles with item pictures flicking around inside them. Drive through one to get a random item, then press E to use it.
4 · ITEMS

    Which items you get depends on your position. If you're leading you mostly get defensive ones. If you're behind you get the ones that help you catch up. Rivals use items too.

    5 · WIN

    Finish 3 laps ahead of the other 5 karts. Each world has its own ladder: win to unlock the next difficulty, from Rookie up through Pro and Expert to Champion. On the harder levels, rivals use turbos out of corners as well, so you'll need to drift every corner.
